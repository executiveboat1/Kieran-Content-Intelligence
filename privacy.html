Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who Operates This Application

Kieran Content Intelligence is a private content workflow for Kieran’s authorized team. The operator can be contacted at executiveboat1@gmail.com. This website is informational and does not provide public account registration or a TikTok connection service.

2. Information Processed

The local workflow stores videos and audio, images and media observations, available transcripts, captions and hooks, editorial choices and feedback, imported social performance records, local preparation packages, scheduling plans, and operational logs. These materials may contain names, likenesses, voices, account identifiers, or other personal information.

Supported account checks use account identifiers, permission scopes, expiry information, and authorization credentials. The current Mac authentication store contains Google client configuration and YouTube access and refresh tokens. Other supported checks can read credentials supplied privately by the operator. Contact requests contain the information the sender provides.

3. Purposes

Information is used to organize content, analyze available media, draft and review captions and hooks, preserve editorial decisions, prepare local output and plans, check authorized accounts, troubleshoot failures, and respond to requests. Approval of content does not by itself authorize an upload or publication.

4. TikTok Status and Account Control

The deployed local application does not currently implement TikTok OAuth, store a configured TikTok account grant, or send content to TikTok. A local TikTok preparation plan is not an authorized account connection. Proposed TikTok functionality is not active.

For existing supported connections, permission can be withdrawn using the relevant provider’s account controls. Withdrawing provider permission does not automatically erase local files, imported content, logs, or backups. Contact the operator to request local disconnection or deletion. A future TikTok integration will require a working authorization and revocation process and updated disclosures before it processes TikTok account data.

5. Storage and Security

Project media, databases, feedback, logs, backups, and engineering records are held on the operator’s Mac and external drive, Lily. The Mac’s internal storage has FileVault enabled. The external drive uses ExFAT with ownership permissions disabled; reported folder modes on that drive do not provide Mac-style per-user access protection. External-drive encryption has not been established.

OAuth credentials for the implemented local account checks are stored as plaintext JSON in an owner-restricted Mac folder, outside the project and public website. The application checks file ownership, permissions, file type, links, and size, and uses atomic credential replacement. It does not apply separate application-level encryption to these files. Readiness signatures check authenticity and do not encrypt data. Local review endpoints use loopback, session, and request-origin checks. These controls reduce risk but do not guarantee security.

6. External Processing

OpenAI/Codex processes project material supplied to the AI-assisted conversation, including selected media evidence, captions, and feedback. Google/YouTube processes supported authentication and account-check requests. GitHub Pages hosts this public website and may process website-request information under GitHub’s own policies. Requests sent to our contact email are handled through Google’s email service.

A configured SSH cloud-worker route can transfer project media and analysis or review results to a remote Kieran publishing host. The local cloud queue is currently paused. Earlier local transfer records exist; the remote host’s current contents, hosting-provider identity, and deletion or backup practices have not been verified. The Metricool connector’s inspected brand currently has no connected social networks. This does not establish the absence of data in other provider accounts.

Provider-side retention and account settings are governed by the relevant service and are not controlled by a local file deletion. Local tools such as Whisper and local language or vision models are separate from the OpenAI-assisted conversation. We will update this policy before enabling additional data flows.

7. Retention

Project content, approved and rejected editorial choices, feedback, database backups, and engineering evidence have no automatic project-wide expiry. They remain until the operator carries out an authorized deletion. Backups can retain earlier versions. Temporary-file cleanup and rotating operational logs limit some files but do not erase all personal information. Readiness expiry invalidates a status receipt; it does not delete the receipt or revoke a credential.

There is no deployed automatic account-data purge or verified comprehensive backup-deletion system. No fixed deletion deadline or secure physical erasure guarantee is promised.

8. Access, Disconnection, and Deletion Requests

Send requests to executiveboat1@gmail.com. Identify the account or content and the action requested; do not email passwords, access tokens, or refresh tokens. The operator will confirm identity and scope before changing access or removing information.

The manual procedure is to identify affected active records and copies, confirm any need to preserve approvals, rights records, or other required evidence, obtain authorization for the exact changes, and then verify the outcome. Backups, exports, archived engineering records, and remote-provider copies must be considered separately. Removing one active file is not confirmation that all copies have been erased. If a provider operation fails, a backup cannot be checked, or information must be retained, the operator will report that limitation rather than confirm full deletion.

Disconnection and provider revocation are separate from erasing content already uploaded to a provider. Provider-held data may require a request through that provider.

9. Changes

Updated practices and the date of revision will be published on this page. New account integrations or processing will be disclosed before activation.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, access, account disconnection, or deletion requests, contact executiveboat1@gmail.com.
