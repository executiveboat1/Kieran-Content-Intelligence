Privacy Policy

1. About This Application

Kieran Content Intelligence is a content management application for organizing, preparing, reviewing, and managing short-form video content. TikTok integration features are under development and subject to applicable permissions and approvals.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the application may process:

  • TikTok account identifiers and profile information.
  • Authorization credentials and access tokens.
  • Videos, captions, and publishing instructions.
  • Upload status, errors, and operational records.
  • Information submitted in support requests.

Access to TikTok information is limited to permissions granted by the account holder and functionality available through approved APIs.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and review content, submit permitted uploads, monitor processing status, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support requests.

TikTok information is not obtained for unauthorized account access or unrelated advertising purposes.

4. Account Authorization

TikTok account connections, when enabled, require authorization through TikTok's supported processes. Users can revoke permissions through TikTok's account settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

The application may use local or hosted infrastructure to process content and account information. Access to account information should be restricted to authorized operators.

Security controls, storage arrangements, and credential-handling procedures must be verified against the deployed application before this policy is finalized.

6. Information Sharing

Information may be transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform an authorized integration operation. Infrastructure providers may process information when used to operate the application.

Any additional sharing practices must be reflected in this policy before the relevant functionality is enabled.

7. Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only as necessary for authorized application functions and applicable legal obligations.

Users may request account disconnection or deletion of associated personal information by contacting the application operator.

Specific retention periods, deletion procedures, and any required exceptions must be confirmed before this policy is finalized.

8. Third-Party Services

TikTok integrations are governed by applicable TikTok terms, permissions, and privacy practices. Third-party services may maintain separate policies.
