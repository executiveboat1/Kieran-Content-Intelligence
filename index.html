About the Platform

Kieran Content Intelligence is a content management platform designed to help authorized creators organize short-form videos, prepare captions, review content, and manage publishing workflows.

TikTok Integration

The platform is being developed to support TikTok account authorization, video uploads, and publishing workflows through TikTok's approved APIs. Publishing actions are subject to user authorization, platform permissions, and applicable review requirements.

Human Review

Content is reviewed and approved by an authorized operator before being submitted for publishing.
