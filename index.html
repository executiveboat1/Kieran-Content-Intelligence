About the Application

Kieran Content Intelligence is a content management application designed to help authorized operators organize video assets, prepare captions, review short-form content, and manage publishing workflows.

Content Preparation

The application supports content organization and preparation workflows, including video review, caption drafting, and content staging.

Human Approval

The application is designed around human review and approval before publishing. Automated public publishing is not currently represented as an available feature.

TikTok Integration

TikTok integration is under development. Planned capabilities include authorized account connections, supported video uploads, and publishing management through approved TikTok APIs.

Availability depends on account authorization, developer permissions, platform policies, and applicable TikTok review requirements.
