Kieran Content Intelligence

Short-form video preparation, content review, and local publishing preparation for Kieran’s authorized team.

About the Application

Kieran Content Intelligence is a private content workflow operated on a Mac and external drive. Authorized operators organize video assets, prepare captions, review content, and create local preparation packages and scheduling plans.

This website provides information about the application. It does not provide public registration, account login, or video uploads.

Content Preparation

The workflow combines local media tools with an AI-assisted conversation for caption and hook drafting. Drafts are grounded in available clip evidence and saved editorial feedback. Drafts and local plans require human review; they are not evidence that a post has been uploaded or published.

Human Approval

Account authorization, caption approval, local preparation, and permission to publish are separate decisions. A local scheduling plan does not schedule a post on a social platform. Automated public publishing is not an available feature of the deployed local workflow.

TikTok Integration

The deployed local application does not currently implement TikTok account authorization or TikTok publishing. TikTok plans in the local workspace are preparation records, not connected accounts or remote posts.

The application is an internal team utility. TikTok’s current Direct Post guidelines do not permit a utility limited to uploading to accounts managed by its own team. We do not represent this application as a publicly available creator platform or an approved TikTok integration.

Content can be prepared locally for an authorized operator to review and post using TikTok’s own supported tools. Any future API integration requires an eligible use case, working functionality, account consent, applicable platform approval, and updated disclosures before activation.
